🚀 Our Mission & Why PrepSelf Was Built

Every year, over 30 million students in India appear for competitive recruitment examinations — from Banking (SBI, IBPS, RBI) and Civil Services (UPSC CSE, State PSCs) to Staff Selection (SSC CGL/CHSL), Railways (RRB NTPC/Group D), Defence (NDA/CDS), and National Entrances (CUET, CLAT, CAT, GATE, NEET, JEE).

Unfortunately, high-quality exam coaching has become increasingly commercialized, with institutes charging anywhere between ₹40,000 and ₹1,50,000 for foundation batches and mock test series. This creates an unfair divide that severely penalizes hardworking aspirants from tier-2, tier-3 cities, and rural India.

PrepSelf was created to dismantle that paywall. By pairing modern web technology with syllabus-calibrated AI prompt engineering, we give every self-studying student the exact capabilities of a personal 24/7 super-tutor: generating authentic PYQ drills, explaining complex arithmetic visually, grading descriptive essays, and mapping out daily study schedules — completely free of charge.

Yes. PrepSelf is 100% free and open-access. You do not need to enter credit card details, pay for unlock codes, or purchase test series passes. All 27 study modules, roadmaps, and prompt tools are un-gated.
