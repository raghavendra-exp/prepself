📋 Strategic Overview & Recruitment Mandate

The UPSC Civil Services Examination is India's premier public recruitment exam. Structured across three successive filters—Preliminary Examination (Objective), Main Examination (Written Descriptive), and Personality Test (Interview)—it tests conceptual depth, ethical clarity, and analytical synthesis across diverse disciplines.

⚖️ Official Examination Pattern & Marking Scheme

🔹 Stage 1: Preliminary Examination (CBT / Screening)

General Studies Paper-I: 100 Qs / 200 Marks (determines Prelims cutoff). General Studies Paper-II (CSAT): 80 Qs / 200 Marks (strictly qualifying at 33% = 66 marks). Negative marking: 1/3rd (0.66 marks for GS-1, 0.83 marks for CSAT).

🔹 Stage 2: Main Examination (Merit Determination)

Written Stage (1750 Marks): 1 Essay Paper (250 M), 4 General Studies Papers (GS 1 to 4, 250 M each = 1000 M), and 2 Optional Papers (250 M each = 500 M). Plus 2 Qualifying Language Papers (300 M each).

🗺️ 4-Phase Battle-Tested Preparation Roadmap

Phase 1: NCERT Foundation & Core Reading (Months 1–4)

Read NCERTs (Classes 6 to 12) in History, Geography, Polity, and Economics. Establish strong fundamental concepts.

Phase 2: Standard References & Optional Mastery (Months 5–8)

Complete Laxmikanth (Polity), Spectrum (Modern History), Nitin Singhania (Art & Culture), and finish Paper 1 & 2 of your chosen Optional subject.

Phase 3: Mains Answer Writing & Ethics Lab (Months 9–10)

Practice 2 GS answers daily. Solve 50+ ethics case studies from GS-4 with stakeholder analysis and moral philosophy frameworks.

Phase 4: Prelims Intensive & CSAT Lockdown (Months 11–12)

Solve 40 full-length GS-1 test papers and practice 500+ CSAT reading comprehension passages to guarantee qualifying well above 66 marks.
