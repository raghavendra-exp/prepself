📋 Strategic Overview & Recruitment Mandate

The State Bank of India conducts annual recruitment for Probationary Officers (SBI PO) and Junior Associates (SBI Clerk). Unlike IBPS examinations, SBI PO does not have sectional cutoffs in Prelims and Mains, but enforces strict category-wise overall cutoffs and a highly competitive psychometric and interview phase.

⚖️ Official Examination Pattern & Marking Scheme

🔹 Stage 1: Preliminary Examination (CBT / Screening)

100 Marks (English: 30 Qs / 20 mins, Quant: 35 Qs / 20 mins, Reasoning: 35 Qs / 20 mins). Negative marking: 0.25 marks per wrong answer.

🔹 Stage 2: Main Examination (Merit Determination)

SBI PO Mains: 200 Marks Objective (Reasoning & Computer: 40 Qs, Data Analysis & Interpretation: 30 Qs, General/Banking Awareness: 50 Qs, English: 35 Qs) + 50 Marks Descriptive Test (Letter & Essay). SBI Clerk Mains: 200 Marks Objective in 160 minutes.

🗺️ 4-Phase Battle-Tested Preparation Roadmap

Phase 1: Speed Arithmetic & Baselines (Days 1–25)

Master fraction-percentage equivalents (1/2 to 1/20), Vedic math square roots, multiplication ladders, and quadratic sign techniques.

Phase 2: Complex Puzzles & Advanced DI (Days 26–55)

Tackle 8-floor, flat-based, and variable-attribute seating puzzles. Practice Caselet, Missing Data, and Radar DI sets.

Phase 3: Sectional Time-Cracking & Speed Drills (Days 56–70)

Enforce strict 20-minute section drills. Learn to skip lengthy arithmetic on first pass and lock in 28+ marks in 18 minutes.

Phase 4: Full-Length Mocks & Mistake Notebook (Days 71–84)

Simulate real exam pressure every 48 hours. Review errors in your Mistake Notebook using spaced repetition before exam day.
