Current Affairs Capsule & Banking Awareness (2026–2027)

📅 Edition: October 2026 🎯 Target: SBI PO, IBPS, SSC CGL & UPSC ✅ Curated by Raghavbegins 📖 10-Minute Read

🏦 1. Banking, RBI Directives & Financial Sector

RBI Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) Bi-Monthly Review

The MPC maintained the benchmark Policy Repo Rate at 6.50% to align headline retail inflation with the 4% medium-term target. Standing Deposit Facility (SDF) rate stands at 6.25% and Marginal Standing Facility (MSF) rate at 6.75%.

Central Bank Digital Currency (CBDC / e-Rupee) Interoperability

RBI expanded retail e-Rupee functionality by integrating offline merchant payments via NFC (Near Field Communication) and programmable tokens for targeted agricultural credit disbursements.

Prompt Corrective Action (PCA) Framework Updates

Revised guidelines enforce strict capital adequacy and net NPA thresholds for upper-tier Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFC-UL) to preempt contagion risk across the shadow banking ecosystem.

🏛️ 2. National Initiatives & Government Schemes

PM-Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana Milestone

The rooftop solar installation scheme surpassed 1.5 million household registrations, offering collateral-free low-interest bank financing up to ₹2,00,000 subsidized by the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy.

India Semiconductor Mission (ISM) Phase-2

The Union Cabinet approved three additional advanced packaging and fabrication facilities in Gujarat and Assam, scaling domestic commercial fab operations under the modified 50% capital support scheme.

📈 3. Economy, Trade & Multilateral Agreements

Goods and Services Tax (GST) Collection Milestones

Gross GST revenue averaged ₹1.85 lakh crore monthly, driven by intensified electronic invoicing compliance and robust domestic consumption during festive quarters.

India-EFTA Trade and Economic Partnership Agreement (TEPA)

Ratification milestones progressed toward the $100 billion committed investment target over 15 years from Switzerland, Norway, Iceland, and Liechtenstein.

🎯 5-Question Capsule Revision Drill

Test your retention of this month's critical policy and economic milestones.

1. What is the current spread between the Standing Deposit Facility (SDF) and the Marginal Standing Facility (MSF) under RBI's LAF corridor?
💡 Explanation: SDF is 25 bps below Repo, and MSF is 25 bps above Repo. Total corridor width = 25 + 25 = 50 bps (0.50%).
2. Which four nations comprise the European Free Trade Association (EFTA) with which India signed the TEPA pact?
💡 Explanation: EFTA is an intergovernmental organisation of Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway, and Switzerland.
3. What is the maximum subsidy support percentage offered under the modified India Semiconductor Mission (ISM) for setting up silicon fabs?
💡 Explanation: The Union Government provides 50% fiscal support on a pari-passu basis for semiconductor and display fabrication units.
4. Which regulatory metric determines inclusion of Non-Banking Financial Companies in the Upper Layer (NBFC-UL) for PCA supervision?
💡 Explanation: RBI classifies NBFCs into Base, Middle, Upper, and Top layers based on asset size, interconnectedness, and parametric systemic impact.
5. What is the statutory minimum capital adequacy ratio (CRAR) mandated for Scheduled Commercial Banks under Basel III norms in India?
💡 Explanation: RBI requires commercial banks in India to maintain a minimum CRAR of 9%, plus a CCB of 2.5%, totaling 11.5%.
