🔬 How Normalization Works in Multi-Shift Exams

In exams conducted across multiple shifts (like SSC CGL, RRB NTPC, and IBPS), paper difficulty varies. Commissions use standard deviation formulas or percentile equi-percentile equi-percentile methods to ensure fair parity:

  • Tougher shifts receive score increments to compensate for difficulty.
  • Easier shifts see moderation in raw scores.
  • Accuracy plays a vital role in tie-breaking in normalization algorithms.
