📐 Negative Marking Formula Guide

$$\text{Net Score} = (\text{Correct Answers} \times \text{Marks per Question}) - (\text{Wrong Answers} \times \text{Negative Penalty})$$

Why guessing is risky: If an exam charges a 0.25 penalty on 1 mark (25%), 4 blind guesses yielding 1 right answer results in 0 net gain ($1.0 - (3 \times 0.25) = +0.25$). However, when accuracy falls below 25%, negative marking severely reduces your merit score.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions: Negative Marking

How is negative marking calculated in SSC CGL Tier 1? In SSC CGL Tier 1, each question carries 2 marks. For every incorrect response, 0.50 marks (25% of the question value) are deducted from your total score.
What is the negative marking penalty in Banking exams (SBI PO, IBPS)? In SBI PO and IBPS exams, each correct question awards 1 mark, while each wrong answer deducts 0.25 marks (1/4th penalty). Unattempted questions carry zero penalty.
How does negative marking work in UPSC CSE Prelims GS 1? UPSC Prelims GS Paper 1 has 100 questions of 2 marks each (total 200 marks). Each wrong answer incurs a 1/3rd penalty (-0.66 marks).
