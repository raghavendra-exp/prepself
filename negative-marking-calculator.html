📐 Negative Marking Formula Guide

$$\text{Net Score} = (\text{Correct Answers} \times \text{Marks per Question}) - (\text{Wrong Answers} \times \text{Negative Penalty})$$

Why guessing is risky: If an exam charges a 0.25 penalty on 1 mark (25%), 4 blind guesses yielding 1 right answer results in 0 net gain ($1.0 - (3 \times 0.25) = +0.25$). However, when accuracy falls below 25%, negative marking severely reduces your merit score.
