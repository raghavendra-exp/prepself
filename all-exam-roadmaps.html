🏦 Banking Sector

SBI PO, IBPS PO, RRB & RBI Grade B Roadmap

Speed-intensive preparation combining 45-second calculation shortcuts, high-difficulty puzzle cases, and financial awareness.

1
Speed Math & Core Concepts
Weeks 1 – 4 (Month 1)
  • Learn tables up to 30, squares up to 50, cubes up to 30, fraction to percentage.
  • Master Simplification, Approximation, Number Series, Quadratic Comparisons.
  • Grammar essentials: Subject-Verb Agreement, Prepositions, Tenses.
  • Reasoning basics: Syllogisms, Inequalities, Direction Sense, Blood Relations.
2
Arithmetic & Puzzle Mastery
Weeks 5 – 10 (Months 2–3)
  • Arithmetic topics: Percentages, Profit & Loss, Ratio, SI-CI, Time & Work, TSD.
  • Data Interpretation: Tables, Bar, Line, Pie Charts, Missing DI, Caselets.
  • Puzzles: Circular, Linear (Parallel/Single), Floor, Box, Month-Day arrangements.
  • Daily Reading Comprehension & Editorial analysis from The Hindu / Indian Express.
3
Prelims Speed & Accuracy Mocks
Weeks 11 – 16 (Month 4)
  • Attempt 1 full-length Prelims mock every alternate day (60 minutes).
  • Conduct thorough 90-minute mock post-mortem: categorize every missed question.
  • Target 65+ net score in PO Prelims and 78+ in Clerk Prelims.
  • Initiate daily Banking Awareness & RBI Monetary Policy study.
4
Mains Deep-Dive & Current Affairs
Weeks 17 – 24 (Months 5–6)
  • High-level Mains DI (Radar, Funnel, Mixed, Arithmetic-based DI).
  • Advanced Critical Reasoning: Cause & Effect, Course of Action, Assumptions.
  • Revise 6 months of Current Affairs & Financial news with monthly capsules.
  • Weekly descriptive essay & formal letter typing on computer keyboard.

🏛️ Civil Services

UPSC CSE (IAS / IPS / IFS) Step-by-Step Guide

Holistic 12-month integrated preparation covering Prelims GS & CSAT, Mains GS 1–4, Essay writing, and Optional subject mastery.

1
NCERT Base & Syllabus Mapping
Months 1 – 3 (Foundations)
  • Read Class 6–12 NCERTs for History, Geography, Polity, Economy, Science.
  • Internalize the UPSC Mains syllabus line-by-line; create subject dossiers.
  • Start daily reading of The Hindu / The Indian Express with issue-based notes.
  • Select your Optional Subject based on background, interest, and syllabus overlap.
2
Standard Texts & Optional Phase 1
Months 4 – 7 (Core Build)
  • Complete Laxmikanth (Polity), Spectrum (Modern History), Shankar (Environment).
  • Optional Subject: Finish 50–60% of Paper 1 and Paper 2 syllabus with notes.
  • Begin weekly Mains answer writing (2 questions daily with model structure).
  • Track monthly government reports: NITI Aayog, Economic Survey, Budget summary.
3
Mains Focus & Ethics Dossier
Months 8 – 10 (Consolidation)
  • Complete GS-4 Ethics, Integrity & Aptitude case studies & thinker frameworks.
  • Write 8 to 10 full-length Mains Essays (philosophical, socio-economic, technological).
  • Complete Optional subject with 10 years solved previous question papers.
  • Synthesize current affairs into micro-diagrams, statistics, and Supreme Court rulings.
4
Exclusive Prelims Mode & CSAT
Months 11 – 12 (Last 90 Days)
  • Solve 40+ Prelims GS Mock Tests; master option elimination techniques.
  • CSAT Daily Drill: Practice 20 Reading Comprehensions + 15 Quant/Reasoning items.
  • Revise high-yield static areas: Modern History timelines, National Parks, Ramsar sites.
  • Maintain calm composure and calibrate biological clock for 09:30 AM exam slot.
📚 Mains Answer Writing Formula (Introduction – Body – Conclusion)

Introduction (15–20% words): Define key terms, cite constitutional articles (e.g. Art. 21, 32), or provide a recent authoritative report/data point.

Body (65–70% words): Multi-dimensional analysis using PESTLE framework (Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Legal, Environmental). Use subheadings & flowcharts.

Way Forward & Conclusion (15% words): Constructive, forward-looking, solution-oriented conclusion aligned with constitutional values and SDG targets.

CSAT Strategy: Never neglect CSAT Paper-2! Dedicate 45 minutes daily to avoid disqualification on the 33% qualifying threshold.

SSC CGL, CHSL, MTS & CPO Preparation Strategy

Master Tier-1 qualifying and Tier-2 merit sections: Advanced Maths, Logical Reasoning, High-Frequency Vocab, General Awareness, and Computer Proficiency.

1
Geometry, Algebra & Grammar
Weeks 1 – 6 (Foundation)
  • Advanced Maths: Geometry theorems, Trigonometry identities, Mensuration 2D/3D.
  • Algebra: Polynomials, quadratic equations, factorization shortcuts.
  • English Grammar: Active/Passive Voice, Direct/Indirect Speech, Idioms & Phrases.
  • Lucent GK: Static Science, Ancient/Medieval/Modern History, Indian Polity.
2
TCS PYQ Solving Machine
Weeks 7 – 12 (Pattern Drills)
  • Solve 5 years of TCS shift papers (2020–2025) chapter by chapter.
  • Identify repeated question archetypes in Non-Verbal Reasoning & Matrices.
  • Daily One-Word Substitutions and high-frequency spelling tests.
  • Static GK: Folk Dances, Musical Instruments, Census 2011, National Parks.
3
Tier-1 Speed & Accuracy Sprints
Weeks 13 – 16 (Pre-Exam)
  • Daily 1 full-length mock test: 100 questions in 60 minutes.
  • Time Allocation: Reasoning (15m), GK (7m), English (10m), Maths (25m), Review (3m).
  • Eliminate unforced negative marking errors; target 145+ raw score in CGL Tier-1.
4
Tier-2 Merit & Computer Qualifying
Post Tier-1 (Weeks 17–24)
  • Tier-2 Section 1: Maths (30 Qs) + Reasoning (30 Qs) – target 165+/180.
  • Tier-2 Section 2: English (45 Qs) + GK (25 Qs) – target 170+/210.
  • Computer Knowledge Module: OS, MS Office, Networking, Cyber Security (qualifying).
  • Daily 15-minute Typing Practice: target 32+ WPM with 95%+ accuracy.

GATE & PSU Recruitment (CS, ME, EE, EC, CE)

Rigorous analytical preparation targeting Engineering Mathematics (13 marks), General Aptitude (15 marks), and Technical Core (72 marks).

1
Maths & Fundamental Subjects
Months 1 – 3
  • Engineering Maths: Linear Algebra, Calculus, Probability & Statistics, Differential Eq.
  • Complete 2 foundational core technical subjects with standard textbook derivations.
  • Maintain a dedicated 200-page formula notebook with units and boundary conditions.
2
Complete Technical Core Syllabus
Months 4 – 6
  • Complete remaining 8 to 10 technical subjects.
  • Solve 30 years of chapter-wise GATE previous question papers.
  • Practice MSQs (Multiple Select Questions) and NAT (Numerical Answer Type) traps.
3
Subject-Wise & Multi-Subject Tests
Months 7 – 8
  • Enroll in national test series (Made Easy / ACE Academy).
  • Master the virtual scientific calculator interface (no physical calculator allowed!).
  • Analyze test reports: check time spent per 2-mark question (ideal: 2.5 to 3.5 min).
4
Full Length Mock Tests & Revision
Months 9 – 10
  • Attempt 15 to 20 full-length 3-hour mock tests in official GATE exam slots.
  • Target 70+ marks for PSU calls (ONGC, IOCL, NTPC, BHEL, ISRO, BARC).
  • Revise short notes every weekend.

NDA, CDS & AFCAT Comprehensive Roadmap

Two-stage selection: Stage-1 Written Examination (Maths, English, GK) and Stage-2 5-Day SSB Interview (Psychological, GTO, Personal Interview).

1
Written Exam Core Subjects
Months 1 – 3
  • NDA: Class 11-12 Mathematics (Calculus, Trigonometry, Vectors, Matrices).
  • CDS: Elementary Mathematics, English Grammar/Vocab, and General Knowledge.
  • Daily physical fitness: 2.4 km running, pushups, pullups, core strength.
2
Current Affairs & GAT Preparation
Months 4 – 5
  • Defence technology, military exercises, international geopolitical security.
  • Physical geography, modern Indian history, Indian constitution.
  • Solve last 10 years of UPSC NDA/CDS official question papers.
3
SSB Interview Psychological Tests
Post Written Exam
  • TAT (Thematic Apperception Test): Write positive, action-oriented stories.
  • WAT (Word Association Test): Practice 60 words in 15 seconds each.
  • SRT (Situation Reaction Test): Respond with practical, courageous decisions.
4
GTO & Personal Interview Polish
Final Stage
  • Group Discussion (GD) & Lecturette: Speak with structured confidence.
  • GTO tasks: Command task, PGT, HGT, Snake race teamwork ethics.
  • PI: In-depth knowledge of PIQ (Personal Information Questionnaire) form.
