Constitutional Framework & Judicial Doctrines 15-Marker

Structure a model 250-word answer for the question: "[Insert Question, e.g., 'Examine the significance of the Doctrine of Basic Structure in preserving constitutional democracy in India.']. Structure requirements: 1. Introduction (30 words): Define the doctrine with origin case laws (Kesavananda Bharati, 1973) and constitutional rationale. 2. Core Pillars (60 words): 4 key components protected under the doctrine. 3. Critical Analysis (100 words): Arguments for judicial overreach vs. democratic checks and balances. 4. Way Forward & Conclusion (60 words): Balanced synthesis highlighting institutional harmony. 5. Provide 2 schematic flowchart ideas to visualize the evolution.

Inclusive Growth & Macroeconomic Policy Diagnosis
