💡 Exam-Calibrated Solved Problem (PYQ Walkthrough)

Step-by-step topper methodology and elimination shortcuts for recurring question types.

RECURRING QUESTION PATTERN SBI Clerk Mains
Identify the error: 'Neither the manager nor the assistant directors was aware of the policy revision announced yesterday.'
  1. Identify correlative conjunction: 'Neither... nor'.
  2. Rule of Proximity: In 'Neither A nor B', the verb agrees with the subject closest to it (subject B).
  3. Here subject B is 'the assistant directors' which is plural.
  4. The verb used is 'was' (singular), which is grammatically incorrect.
  5. Correction: Change 'was aware' to 'were aware'.
⚡ Topper Speed Strategy: Master the 100 golden rules of grammar, particularly proximity rules with 'neither/nor', 'either/or', and subject-verb inversion.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (Exam Strategy)

How do I improve reading speed for bank exam RCs?

Read one financial editorial daily from The Hindu or The Indian Express. Focus on chunking phrases rather than reading word-by-word, and practice summarizing the central theme in 2 sentences.

How are marks awarded in the SBI PO Descriptive test?

Descriptive English is computer-evaluated on grammatical accuracy, vocabulary breadth, format compliance (formal letter structure), and logical coherence within 250 words.
