💡 Exam-Calibrated Solved Problem (PYQ Walkthrough)

Step-by-step topper methodology and elimination shortcuts for recurring question types.

RECURRING QUESTION PATTERN IBPS PO Mains
Under RBI guidelines, what is the minimum Capital to Risk-Weighted Assets Ratio (CRAR) that scheduled commercial banks in India are required to maintain?
  1. Recall Basel III international guideline: 8% minimum CRAR.
  2. Recall RBI's stricter regulatory mandate for Indian Scheduled Commercial Banks: 9% minimum CRAR.
  3. For Public Sector Banks, RBI additionally mandates a Capital Conservation Buffer (CCB) of 2.5%, taking total Tier 1 + Tier 2 capital requirement to 11.5%.
  4. Final Answer: 9% (or 11.5% including CCB).
⚡ Topper Speed Strategy: Always distinguish between international Basel III minimums (8%) and RBI's conservative Indian regulatory threshold (9% + 2.5% CCB).

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (Exam Strategy)

Is banking awareness asked in Prelims?

No. General and Banking Awareness is tested exclusively in the Mains examination and during the final Personal Interview / Group Discussion stage.

How many months of financial news should I cover?

Cover the last 5 to 6 months leading up to the exam date, with maximum emphasis on RBI circulars, union budget allocations, and key economic surveys.
