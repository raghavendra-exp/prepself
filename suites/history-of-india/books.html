📘 NCERT Foundation

Our Pasts (Parts I, II, & III)

NCERT Class 6, 7, and 8
Target: UPSC Prelims • Archaeological Foundations • Early Feudal Roots

The indispensable gateway to Indian history. Builds foundational clarity on pre-historic hunter-gatherers, the earliest cities (Harappa), janapadas, Ashokan statecraft, early medieval temples, Delhi Sultanate, Mughal administrative nobility, and the arrival of the East India Company.

Core UPSC Concepts & Key Themes

  • Class 6: Burzahom pit-dwellings, Mehrgarh neolithic farming, Dholavira & Lothal dockyard, Vedic Jana vs Janapada.
  • Class 7: Chola Kudavolai village elections, Mansabdari & Jagirdari under Akbar, Shahna-i-Mandi, Bhakti saint-poets.
  • Class 8: Battle of Plassey & Buxar, 1857 Revolt epicenters, De-industrialization of traditional handlooms, Wood's Despatch.

India & the Contemporary World (I & II)

NCERT Class 9 and 10

Pivotal textbook for conceptualizing world history revolutions and global modernities. Covers the French Revolution, the Russian Revolution, the rise of Nazism, the print culture revolution, and European nationalism that sparked anti-colonial resistance across Asia.

Core UPSC Concepts & Key Themes

  • Class 9: Three Estates of Ancien Régime, Jacobin Club & Robespierre, Bolshevik April Theses, Weimar hyperinflation, Nuremberg laws.
  • Class 10: Mazzini & Cavour in Italian Unification, Bismarck's Blood & Iron, Swadeshi & Non-Cooperation, Gutenberg press impact.

📜 Advanced Themes

Themes in Indian History (Parts I, II, & III)

NCERT Class 11 and 12
Target: Direct UPSC Prelims Questions • Primary Sources • Historiography

The single most heavily tested textbook series in the UPSC Preliminary Examination over the last 15 years. Uses contemporary archaeological reports, epigraphic transcriptions, foreign traveler accounts, and colonial gazetteers to provide profound thematic depth.

Core UPSC Concepts & Key Themes

  • Theme 1–4: Bricks, Beads and Bones (Harappan craft); Kings, Farmers and Towns (Mauryan epigraphy); Kinship & Caste (Mahabharata); Stupas & Ajanta murals.
  • Theme 5–9: Al-Biruni's Kitab-ul-Hind, Ibn Battuta's Rihla, Bhakti-Sufi traditions (Alvars, Nayanars, Virashaivas), Vijayanagara urban core & Mahanavami Dibba, Ain-i-Akbari.
  • Theme 10–15: Permanent Settlement & Fifth Report, Santhal Hool, 1857 proclamations, Mahatma Gandhi & mass civil disobedience, Constituent Assembly debates.

🏺 Ancient Benchmark

India's Ancient Past

Prof. R.S. Sharma (Oxford University Press)
Target: Chronological Rigour • Second Urbanization • Ancient Material Culture

The definitive standard textbook for building an unbreakable chronological and socio-economic timeline of Ancient India. Focuses on the material foundations of historical change: iron technology, agricultural surplus, punch-marked coinage, and trade guild emergence.

Core UPSC Concepts & Key Themes

  • Paleolithic to Chalcolithic transitions (Bhimbetka, Inamgaon, Jorwe culture).
  • Second Urbanization in the Gangetic Basin & the rise of Magadha's 16 Mahajanapadas.
  • Mauryan centralized administrative statecraft, Ashoka's Dhamma, and post-Mauryan trade (Kushana gold dinaras & Satavahana maritime networks).
  • Gupta classical golden age, land grant economy (Agraharas), and early Indian feudalism.

🏛️ Advanced Ancient

History of Ancient & Early Medieval India

Prof. Upinder Singh (Pearson)
Target: Conceptual Nuance • Inscriptions & Epigraphy • Cultural Transitions

The gold standard for advanced UPSC Mains answer writing and conceptual Prelims traps. Synthesizes archaeological excavations, primary epigraphic inscriptions, literary texts, and trans-regional cultural exchanges from the Stone Age to the 12th century CE.

Core UPSC Concepts & Key Themes

  • Epigraphic analysis of Ashokan Major & Minor Rock Edicts across Greek, Aramaic, Prakrit.
  • Megalithic burial traditions of South India and Sangam socio-political landscapes.
  • Architectural evolution of rock-cut caves (Barabar, Karle, Kanheri, Ajanta, Ellora).
  • Peninsular dynasties: Pallavas of Kanchi, Early Chalukyas of Badami, Rashtrakutas, and Imperial Chola administrative agrarian institutions.

⚔️ Medieval Masterwork

History of Medieval India

Prof. Satish Chandra (Orient BlackSwan)
Target: Delhi Sultanate • Mughal Administration • Peninsular Empires

The canonical textbook for Medieval India. Provides lucid, balanced political, agrarian, and cultural analysis of the Delhi Sultanate, the Vijayanagara Empire, the Bahmani kingdoms, the Mughal Empire, and the rise of the Maratha confederacy under Chhatrapati Shivaji.

Core UPSC Concepts & Key Themes

  • Tripartite Struggle for Kannauj (Gurjara-Pratiharas, Palas, Rashtrakutas).
  • Delhi Sultanate administrative machinery: Iqta system, Balban's blood and iron, Alauddin Khilji's market control regulations (Shahna-i-Mandi), and Tughlaq reforms.
  • Mughal imperial state: Akbar's Sulh-i-Kul, Mansabdari rank system (Zat & Sawar), Dahsala land revenue settlement, and Jagirdari agrarian crisis.
  • Chhatrapati Shivaji's Ashtapradhan council, Chauth & Sardeshmukhi levies.

📖 Modern High-Yield

A Brief History of Modern India

Rajiv Ahir, IPS (Spectrum Publications)
Target: UPSC Prelims High-Yield Facts • Chronological Precision • Acts & Treaties

The most widely read quick-revision textbook for Modern Indian History. Covers the advent of European trading companies, colonial constitutional enactments (1773 to 1947), socio-religious reform societies, tribal and peasant uprisings, and phase-by-phase national movement milestones.

Core UPSC Concepts & Key Themes

  • Colonial expansionist doctrines: Subsidiary Alliance (Wellesley) & Doctrine of Lapse (Dalhousie).
  • Socio-religious reform movements: Brahmo Samaj, Arya Samaj, Ramakrishna Mission, Satyashodhak Samaj, Aligarh Movement.
  • Constitutional development: Regulating Act 1773, Charter Acts, Indian Councils Acts 1861/1892, Morley-Minto 1909, Montagu-Chelmsford 1919, GoI Act 1935.
  • Swadeshi movement, Non-Cooperation, Civil Disobedience, Cripps Mission, Quit India, Cabinet Mission 1946.

🇮🇳 Mains Analytical

India's Struggle for Independence

Prof. Bipan Chandra et al. (Penguin)
Target: UPSC GS-1 Mains Analytical Architect • Mass Nationalism Dynamics

The foundational analytical masterpiece for UPSC Mains GS Paper-1. Decodes the character of the colonial state, the Gramscian hegemonic nature of British rule, Gandhi's Struggle-Truce-Struggle (S-T-S) strategy, revolutionary socialism of Bhagat Singh, and peasant-working class mobilizations.

Core UPSC Concepts & Key Themes

  • Economic critique of colonialism: Dadabhai Naoroji, R.C. Dutt, and M.G. Ranade's Drain Theory.
  • Character of the Moderates vs Extremists, and the strategic significance of the 1907 Surat Split.
  • Mahatma Gandhi's moral and ideological synthesis: Satyagraha, Ahimsa, Harijan upliftment, and constructive programs.
  • Revolutionary socialism (HRA, HSRA, Kakori, Bhagat Singh) and the socio-economic radicalization of the 1930s.

🎨 Art & Heritage

Indian Art and Culture

Nitin Singhania, IAS (McGraw Hill)
Target: UPSC Prelims & Mains Visual Arts, Performing Arts & UNESCO Heritage

The encyclopedic reference handbook for Indian art, architecture, performing arts, and religious philosophies. Extensively illustrates temple architectures (Nagara, Dravida, Vesara), mural and miniature paintings, classical dances, music traditions, UNESCO Tangible & Intangible Heritage elements, and regional handicrafts.

Core UPSC Concepts & Key Themes

  • Temple architecture: Garbhagriha, Shikhara, Vimana, Mandapa, Gopurams, and Panchayatan layout.
  • Paintings: Murals (Ajanta, Bagh, Sittanavasal) and Miniatures (Mughal, Rajasthani, Pahari, Kangra).
  • Performing Arts: 8 Sangeet Natak Akademi classical dances, Natyashastra instrument classification (Tata, Sushira, Avanaddha, Ghana), Puppetry, and martial arts (Kalaripayattu, Thang-Ta).
  • Indian Philosophy: Six Astika schools (Samkhya, Yoga, Nyaya, Vaisheshika, Mimamsa, Vedanta) & Shramanic traditions.

🌐 World History

Mastering Modern World History

Norman Lowe (Macmillan / Bloomsbury)
Target: UPSC GS-1 World History • International Relations Foundations

The gold-standard reference for Modern World History since the 18th century. Masterfully dissects the Enlightenment, American and French Revolutions, the Industrial Revolution, the Scramble for Africa, World Wars I and II, the League of Nations, the Great Depression, the Cold War, and post-WWII Decolonization.

Core UPSC Concepts & Key Themes

  • 18th Century Revolutions: Locke & Montesquieu, Declaration of Independence 1776, French Estates-General 1789, Napoleonic Code.
  • 19th Century Transformations: Industrial Revolution in Britain, Scramble for Africa & Berlin Conference 1884, German & Italian Unifications.
  • 20th Century Cataclysms: Causes of WWI, Treaty of Versailles 1919 (Diktat), Russian Bolshevik Revolution 1917, Rise of Mussolini & Hitler, Great Depression & New Deal.
  • Post-1945 Geopolitics: United Nations formation, Cold War (Containment, Cuban Missile Crisis 1962), Decolonization in Asia/Africa, Bandung Conference & NAM, Collapse of the USSR 1991.

👥 Indian Society Benchmark

Society in India & Social Problems in India

Prof. Ram Ahuja (Rawat Publications)
Target: UPSC GS-1 Indian Society • Social Stratification • Contemporary Social Issues

The canonical standard reference for UPSC GS-1 Indian Society. Thoroughly analyzes the structural features of Indian society, caste system transformations, joint family evolution, tribal integration models, communalism dynamics, regionalism causes, and systemic social challenges.

Core UPSC Concepts & Key Themes

  • Structural Dimensions: Varna vs Jati, caste endogamy, changes in joint family structures, status of women across eras.
  • Social Problems: Communalism causes & stages, regionalism & Sons of the Soil movements, poverty & unemployment nexus.
  • Marginalized Groups: Problems of SCs, STs, OBCs, elderly demographics, domestic violence, and bonded/child labor.
  • Modern Transformations: Secularism in India vs Western models, social deviance, youth unrest, and drug abuse problems.

📊 NCERT Sociology

Indian Society & Social Change and Development in India

NCERT Class 11 & 12 (Sociology Department)
Target: UPSC GS-1 Core Concepts • Demographic Transition • Social Movements

The foundational conceptual textbooks for Indian Society. Delves into demographic structures, social institutions (continuity and change), patterns of social inequality and exclusion, cultural diversity challenges, and structural processes of Sanskritization, Westernization, Modernization, and Secularization.

Core UPSC Concepts & Key Themes

  • Class 12 Indian Society: Demographic structure of India (TFR, Ageing, Dividend), Caste & Tribe institutions, Patterns of social inequality & exclusion (Dalits, Adivasis, Women, Disabled), Challenges of Cultural Diversity.
  • Class 12 Social Change: Structural Change (Colonialism & Urbanization), Cultural Change (Sanskritization, Modernization), Agrarian Structure, Globalization & Social Change, Social Movements (Peasant, Dalit, Women, Environmental).

🏺 Fine Arts Benchmark

An Introduction to Indian Art (Parts I & II)

NCERT Class 11 Fine Arts • CCRT Compendiums
Target: UPSC Prelims Visual Art • Sculpture Architecture • Buddhist Stupas & Temples

The definitive official visual handbook for Indian art history. Provides authentic archaeological photographs, cross-sectional diagrams, and stylistic analysis of prehistoric rock paintings (Bhimbetka), Indus seals, Mauryan pillars, post-Mauryan stupas, Ajanta frescoes, and temple architectures.

Core UPSC Concepts & Key Themes

  • Rock Art & Indus: Upper Palaeolithic and Mesolithic paintings at Bhimbetka, Harappan bronze casting (lost wax), steatite seals, terracotta toys.
  • Sculpture & Architecture: Didarganj Yakshi, Lion Capital of Sarnath, Sanchi Torana narrative carvings, Karle and Kanheri Chaityas.
  • Mural Traditions: Ajanta cave mural techniques (tempera fresco secco), Bodhisattva Padmapani iconography, Ellora Kailash monolithic temple excavation.
  • Temple Forms: Deogarh Dashavatara Gupta temple, Khajuraho Kandariya Mahadeva, Thanjavur Brihadisvara Vimana, Konark Sun Temple.

🇮🇳 Post-Independence Canon

India After Gandhi & India Since Independence

Ramachandra Guha / Prof. Bipan Chandra et al.
Target: UPSC GS-1 Post-Independence Consolidation • Nation Building • Foreign Policy

The master reference texts for Post-Independence Indian History. Illuminates the heroic challenges of partition rehabilitation, 565 princely state integrations (Sardar Patel & V.P. Menon), linguistic state reorganization, Nehruvian democratic institution-building, Green Revolution, 1975 Emergency, and 1991 LPG reforms.

Core UPSC Concepts & Key Themes

  • Nation Building: Integration of Junagadh, Hyderabad (Operation Polo), and Kashmir; Dhar Commission, JVP Committee, and 1956 States Reorganisation Act.
  • Foreign Policy: Non-Aligned Movement (NAM), Panchsheel Principles (1954), 1962 Sino-Indian War, 1965 and 1971 Indo-Pak Wars, Pokhran Nuclear Tests.
  • Agrarian & Economic Reforms: Zamindari Abolition, Bhoodan-Gramdan (Vinoba Bhave), Green Revolution, Operation Flood (AMUL), 1991 LPG crisis and reforms.
  • Polity & Crises: 1975-77 Internal Emergency, JP Total Revolution, Rise of Regional Parties, Coalition Era (1989-2014), Naxalbari uprising.

📜 Advanced Historiography

From Plassey to Partition and After

Prof. Sekhar Bandyopadhyay (Orient BlackSwan)
Target: UPSC Mains Advanced Historiographical Nuance • Subaltern & Elite Perspectives

The premier university-level textbook bridging colonial state formation and nationalist mobilizations. Synthesizes Cambridge, Nationalist, Marxist, and Subaltern historiographical debates regarding British conquest, peasant and tribal insurrections, Gandhian mass movements, caste movements, and the partition holocaust.

Core UPSC Concepts & Key Themes

  • 18th Century Debate: "Dark Age" vs "Prosperity and Reorientation" across successor regional states (Bengal, Awadh, Mysore, Marathas).
  • Colonial Agrarian Economy: Commercialization of agriculture, de-industrialization debate, rural indebtedness, famines.
  • Nationalism Dynamics: Limitations of elite politics, Gramscian hegemonic nature of colonial rule, Subaltern autonomous peasant consciousness.
  • Partition & Independence: Cabinet Mission negotiations, Mountbatten 3rd June Plan, communal mobilization, Radcliffe boundary drawing and human cost.
