Sources, Method & What's New

This site converts a growing set of supplied revision-sheet infographics into an interactive, interconnected reference — a national chronology, a dedicated Uttar Pradesh (UPPSC) track, and a computed knowledge graph linking them together.

What comes from the source sheets

Every period overview, phase, event, aspect-wise table, list of women's contributions, list of important sites, and UPSC Prelims/Mains point on this site is drawn from a corresponding supplied revision sheet, which you can open at any time via the "View Original Revision Sheet" control on each period page. The chronology, terminology, dates, phases and level of detail follow the source sheets; nothing has been invented to make a period look fuller than its source material supports. Where a small number of dates required independent verification (for example, the exact date Uttar Pradesh was renamed in 1950), that verification is noted on the relevant page rather than silently substituted.

What is added for navigation only

Search indexing, the cross-linking between periods/people/themes, the knowledge graph's computed relations (see below), and a small set of curated external reference links (Britannica, UNESCO, Government portals) are added purely as navigation aids and are visually distinguished with an example ↗ external-link style. They are not part of the original source material.

The knowledge graph

Rather than a hand-written list of "related topics," the Knowledge Graph Explorer and every period page's "Related Topics" panel are computed live from the structured fields already in data/history.json — a period's themes, people, women and events arrays. Any name that recurs in two or more periods (for instance, Akbar appearing in both his own national biography page and the Uttar Pradesh Mughal Period page) is automatically cross-linked in both directions, with no manual list to maintain.

The Uttar Pradesh (UPPSC) track

Alongside the national chronology sits a self-contained, 28-page Uttar Pradesh history track aimed at UPPSC's GS Paper I & II — prehistoric U.P. through the Vedic Age, Mahajanapadas, Mauryan and Gupta periods, the medieval Sultanate and Mughal centuries, the Revolt of 1857, British administration, the freedom struggle, and the state's formation, reorganisation and 21st-century development. These pages carry their own region: "up" tag so the main national timeline and home page stay focused on the all-India story, while search, themes, people, women, and the knowledge graph all draw on both tracks together.

Coverage at a glance

The national chronology runs continuously from the Palaeolithic Age through India's present day (2020–Present), with five further pages placing India's colonial and freedom-struggle chapters in global context (the French Revolution, World War I, the Russian Revolution, World War II, and a summary World History chart). The data architecture (data/history.json) is designed so that further periods, deeper sub-pages, or entirely new state-specific tracks can be added by extending the JSON file and dropping in the corresponding infographic, without any redesign. See the project's README.md for the exact steps.
