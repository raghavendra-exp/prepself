Why this exists

Uttar Pradesh has one of the longest, richest cultural sequences of any Indian state — six of the sixteen Mahajanapadas, the Mauryan and Gupta core, Kannauj's imperial centuries, the Delhi Sultanate's Doab heartland, the Mughal capital cities, and the strongest base of the 1857 revolt all sit within its present borders. UPPSC's GS papers draw heavily on this. Every page below follows the same structure as its national counterpart — chronological phases, aspect-wise tables, women's contributions, UPSC Prelims/Mains panels — and is fully wired into the site's search and knowledge graph, so a name like "Akbar" or "Ashoka" here links straight back to its national-level page.
