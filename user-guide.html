📖 Step-by-Step Master Tutorials

Practical instructions for getting the highest accuracy and yield from PrepSelf's AI capabilities.

Tutorial 1: How to Generate 99th-Percentile AI Exam Prompts
  1. Open any prompt generator (e.g. Bank Exam Engine or UPSC Engine).
  2. Select your exact target sub-exam (e.g., SBI PO Mains) and section (e.g., Data Analysis & Interpretation).
  3. Choose the topic (e.g., Missing Caselet DI with Quadratic Roots) and toggle Pedagogical Mode to "Socratic Breakdown with Trap Explanations".
  4. Click Generate Prompt. The engine embeds official marking guidelines, time limits, and difficulty constraints.
  5. Click Copy Prompt, then click ChatGPT or Gemini to paste the prompt. The AI will immediately produce exam-grade drills without generic filler text.
Tutorial 2: How to Use 27 Interactive Study Modules with Dual-Screen Reading
  1. Navigate to Study Modules Hub.
  2. Click on any module card (e.g. Bank Quant Master or UPSC GS-IV Ethics) to open the interactive viewer.
  3. Use the "Switch Module Book" dropdown at the top to access 5 official reference books per vertical.
  4. Click "Toggle Fullscreen" (or press F11) to hide all external browser UI.
  5. On a laptop or tablet, keep your AI chatbot open on the left half of your screen and the PrepSelf Study Module on the right half for seamless interactive problem solving!
Tutorial 3: How to Take Real-Time Mocks with Negative Marking
  1. Open the Mock Quiz Simulator.
  2. Review the timer and instructions (+1 for correct, -0.25 for incorrect).
  3. Answer questions by selecting options. The Question Palette updates in real time (Green = Attempted, Gray = Unvisited).
  4. Click Submit Test to view your detailed scorecard: Total Score, Accuracy Rate, Time Spent, and step-by-step mathematical explanations.
Tutorial 4: How to Install PrepSelf on Mobile (Android & iOS PWA)
  1. Android (Chrome): Open PrepSelf in Chrome. Tap the "Install App" prompt at the top, or tap the three dots (⋮) in the top-right and select "Add to Home screen".
  2. iPhone / iPad (Safari): Open the site in Safari. Tap the Share icon (square with up arrow) and select "Add to Home Screen".
  3. Windows / Mac: In Chrome, Edge, or Brave, click the install icon in the URL bar to launch PrepSelf as a lightweight desktop application!

❓ Frequently Asked Questions & Troubleshooting

Why is PrepSelf completely free? Where is the catch?
There is no catch. PrepSelf is an open-access educational initiative created by Raghavbegins (Raghavendra). As an engineer and educator, the founder believes competitive exam preparation materials should be freely accessible to every student in India regardless of economic background.
What if clicking "ChatGPT" or "Gemini" opens a blank page?
If your browser has an aggressive pop-up blocker, it might block the direct tab launch. Simply click "Copy Prompt", open chatgpt.com or gemini.google.com manually in another tab, and paste the prompt with Ctrl+V.
Can I practice in Hindi or regional languages?
Yes! When generating any prompt, you can add an instruction like "Provide explanations in Hindi (हिन्दी) with bilingual technical terms". All major LLMs (Gemini, ChatGPT) will answer fluently in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, and other Indian languages.
Are the syllabi updated for the 2026–2027 exam cycle?
Yes. Every prompt blueprint and 4-phase roadmap is continuously aligned with the latest recruitment notifications released by SBI, IBPS, UPSC, SSC, Railways RRB, and state public service commissions.
How do I report a typo, issue, or suggest a new exam engine?
You can reach out directly via our Contact Us page or email the creator at raghavendra.contact.mail@gmail.com. We review and implement community suggestions regularly.
