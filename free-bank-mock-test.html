Sample High-Yield Bank Quant & Reasoning Questions

Q1. Quadratic Equation Comparison (SBI PO Prelims Standard)
Equation I: \(x^2 - 15x + 56 = 0\)
Equation II: \(y^2 - 19y + 90 = 0\)
Step 1 (Root Extraction):
For Equation I: Roots add to \(+15\) and multiply to \(+56\). Factor pair is \((7, 8)\). Roots: \(x = +7, +8\).
For Equation II: Roots add to \(+19\) and multiply to \(+90\). Factor pair is \((9, 10)\). Roots: \(y = +9, +10\).
Step 2 (Comparison): Both \(+7\) and \(+8\) are strictly less than both \(+9\) and \(+10\).
Topper Verdict: \(x < y\) in 12 seconds flat.
Q2. Syllogism "Only a Few" Logic (IBPS PO Standard)
Statements: Only a few Laptops are Mobiles. All Mobiles are Tablets.
Conclusions: I. Some Laptops are not Mobiles. II. All Laptops can never be Tablets.
Rule & Analysis:
"Only a few A are B" means two things simultaneously: (1) Some A are B, and (2) Some A are NOT B.
Conclusion I: Directly follows from definition! (True).
Conclusion II: Since all Mobiles are Tablets, can all Laptops fit inside Tablets? Yes, as long as Laptops don't fit entirely inside Mobiles. Therefore, Conclusion II is False.
Topper Verdict: Only Conclusion I follows.

Frequently Asked Questions: Bank Mock Tests

How does sectional timing work in SBI PO and IBPS Clerk Prelims?

Both SBI PO and IBPS Clerk Prelims allocate strictly 20 minutes per section (English: 30 Qs, Quantitative Aptitude: 35 Qs, Reasoning: 35 Qs). You cannot switch between sections until the 20-minute countdown expires.

What is the negative marking deduction in bank mock tests?

Every correct answer awards 1 mark, while each wrong attempt deducts 0.25 marks (1/4th penalty). Unattempted questions carry 0 penalty. In our mock test simulator, negative marks are calculated automatically in your final score.

Are these bank mock tests really 100% free with no login required?

Yes. PrepSelf is an open educational initiative. All full mock tests, speed-math drills, and question solutions are 100% free with no account creation, email sign-up, or credit card required.

What is the recommended question attempt sequence for Bank Quant?

Start with the 'Speed Math Triad' (Simplification/Approximation 5-10 Qs, Quadratic Equations 5 Qs, Number Series 5 Qs) during the first 8-10 minutes. Next, tackle 1-2 standard DI sets (10 Qs in 5-6 minutes). Leave tricky arithmetic word problems for the final 4-5 minutes.
