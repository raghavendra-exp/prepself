Daily 5-Question Speed Drill for Govt Exams

Build a genuine daily habit without burnout. 5 mixed questions across Quant, Reasoning, English, General Awareness, and Computer Aptitude.

Q1 • Quantitative Aptitude • Percentage
If A's salary is 20% more than B's salary, by what percentage is B's salary less than A's salary?
💡 Shortcut Formula: [R / (100 + R)] × 100% = [20 / (100 + 20)] × 100% = 20 / 120 × 100% = ⅙ = 16.66%.
Q2 • Logical Reasoning • Syllogism
Statements: Only a few Pens are Pencils. All Pencils are Erasers. Conclusion: All Pens can never be Pencils.
💡 Rule: 'Only a few Pens are Pencils' implies both 'Some Pens are Pencils' AND 'Some Pens are NOT Pencils'. Hence, all Pens can never be inside Pencils. The conclusion is definitely true.
Q3 • English Language • Error Detection
Identify the error: 'Neither the principal (A) nor the teachers (B) was present (C) in the morning assembly (D).'
💡 Proximity Rule: When two subjects are joined by 'Neither... nor', the verb agrees with the closer subject. 'Teachers' is plural, so the verb must be 'were present'.
Q4 • General Awareness • Indian Economy
What is the upper tolerance limit for headline CPI inflation mandated to RBI under the flexible inflation targeting framework?
💡 RBI Mandate: The inflation target is 4% with a tolerance band of ±2% (Floor = 2%, Ceiling = 6%).
Q5 • Computer Aptitude • Networking
Which network protocol operates at the Transport Layer of the OSI model to ensure reliable, connection-oriented data transfer?
💡 Networking Rule: TCP (Transmission Control Protocol) is connection-oriented and guarantees delivery with 3-way handshakes. UDP is connectionless and unreliable.
