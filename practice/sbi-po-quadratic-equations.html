SBI PO Quadratic Equation Roots Tricks (2026)

📅 Updated: October 2026 for 2026–2027 Pattern ⏱ 5 min read 👨‍🎓 Curated by Raghavbegins ✅ 100% Free Self-Study

The 60-Second Quadratic Inequality Sign Method

Quadratic inequalities form an indispensable 5-mark cluster in SBI PO and IBPS PO Prelims. Toppers regularly harvest all 5 marks in under 90 seconds without ever using the quadratic formula (-b ± √(b² - 4ac)) / 2a. The secret lies in deciphering sign conventions and splitting the middle term by factor recognition.

For an equation in standard format ax² + bx + c = 0, look first at the sign of constant c: If the constant term c is negative in both equations (e.g., x² + 3x - 10 = 0 and y² - 4y - 12 = 0), each equation will produce one positive root and one negative root. In every such scenario, the relationship is automatically 'Cannot be determined' (CND / x = y). You can mark CND in 2 seconds without calculating the roots!

When sign shortcuts do not immediately resolve the equation, use the Sign Inversion Table: If the equation signs are (+, +), both roots are negative (-, -). If signs are (-, +), both roots are positive (+, +). If signs are (+, -), roots are (-, +). If signs are (-, -), roots are (+, -). Compare both roots of x with both roots of y systematically.

Double Negative Constant Rule
If c < 0 in both x and y equations, answer is ALWAYS 'x = y or relation cannot be established'.
⚠️ Common Exam Trap to Avoid: When the leading coefficient 'a' is not 1 (e.g., 2x² - 7x + 6 = 0), aspirants often forget to divide the factor roots by 'a' before comparing with y, causing incorrect inequality decisions.

Q1. Equation I: x² - 7x + 12 = 0 | Equation II: y² - 9y + 20 = 0. What is the relation between x and y?
💡 Step-by-Step Solution: For Eq I: factors of 12 summing to 7 are 3 and 4. Since signs are (-, +), roots are x = +3, +4. For Eq II: factors of 20 summing to 9 are 4 and 5. Roots are y = +4, +5. Comparing: 3 < 4, 3 < 5, 4 = 4, 4 < 5. Hence x ≤ y.
Q2. Equation I: x² + 5x - 24 = 0 | Equation II: y² - 3y - 28 = 0. What is the relation?
💡 Step-by-Step Solution: Both equations have a negative constant term (-24 and -28). Therefore both equations yield one positive and one negative root. An overlap of positive and negative values always produces: relationship cannot be established.
Q3. Equation I: 2x² - 11x + 15 = 0 | Equation II: 2y² - 13y + 21 = 0. Compare x and y.
💡 Step-by-Step Solution: Eq I: Product = 2 × 15 = 30. Factors summing to 11 are 6 and 5. Dividing by a = 2 gives roots: x = +6/2 = 3.0, and +5/2 = 2.5. Eq II: Product = 2 × 21 = 42. Factors summing to 13 are 6 and 7. Roots: y = +6/2 = 3.0, and +7/2 = 3.5. Comparing: 2.5 < 3.0, 2.5 < 3.5, 3.0 = 3.0, 3.0 < 3.5. Hence x ≤ y.
Q4. Equation I: x² = 64 | Equation II: y = √64. Which statement is correct?
💡 Step-by-Step Solution: Crucial distinction: x² = 64 produces x = +8 and x = -8. But the principal square root function y = √64 produces only the non-negative root y = +8. Comparing x (-8, +8) with y (+8): -8 < 8 and 8 = 8. Hence x ≤ y.
Q5. Equation I: x² + 14x + 48 = 0 | Equation II: y² - 10y + 24 = 0. What is the relation?
💡 Step-by-Step Solution: By sign method: Eq I has (+, +), so both roots of x are negative. Eq II has (-, +), so both roots of y are positive. Any negative number is strictly less than any positive number. Therefore x < y without calculating values.
