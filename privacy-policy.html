ℹ️ About This Website

Indian Exam AI Prompt Generator (raghavendra-exp.github.io/PrepSelf) is a free educational platform operated by Raghavbegins, an individual content creator based in India. It provides AI-assisted study prompt tools for 80+ Indian competitive examinations — UPSC, GATE, CLAT, JEE, NEET, Banking, Railway, SSC, Defence, State PSC, Teaching, MBA/CAT, and Law entrance exams.

✅ No registration or login required. All AI prompt generation runs entirely in your browser — no prompt text or personal information is transmitted to our servers.

🇮🇳 Compliance with Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act, India)

PrepSelf complies with the provisions of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) of the Republic of India regarding the processing of digital personal data:

  • Data Fiduciary: Raghavbegins (Raghavendra), Bengaluru, Karnataka, India. Contact: Platform Contact Desk.
  • Lawful Basis & Notice: Personal data (email and name via Firebase) is processed solely upon explicit user consent during optional Google Sign-in to sync study history. You can use 100% of study modules, roadmaps, formulas, and prompt tools without signing in.
  • Right to Access & Correction: Data principals have the right to request a summary of personal data processed and request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Right to Erasure (Account Deletion): You may withdraw consent and request immediate deletion of your synced study history by emailing support or using the in-app Sign Out control.
  • Protection of Children: PrepSelf does not track behavioral profiling of children or serve harmful targeted advertisements to minors.
  • Grievance Redressal: In accordance with DPDP requirements, any data protection queries or complaints may be directed to our Grievance Desk at our Contact Page with acknowledgement within 24 hours.
