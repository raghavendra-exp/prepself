✅ Fast Square of Numbers Ending in 5

Formula: For (N5)², calculate N × (N + 1) and append 25 at the end.

35² = (3 × 4) | 25 = 1225
65² = (6 × 7) | 25 = 4225
95² = (9 × 10) | 25 = 9025
115² = (11 × 12) | 25 = 13225
✅ Base 100 Fast Multiplication

For numbers near 100: (100 + a) × (100 + b) = (100 + a + b) | (a × b)

104 × 107 → (100 + 4 + 7) | (4 × 7) = 11128
106 × 109 → (100 + 6 + 9) | (6 × 9) = 11554
96 × 94 → (-4 × -6 = 24), 96 - 6 = 90 → 9024
✅ Successive Percentage Change (Net Effect)

Net % Change = A + B + (A × B ÷ 100)

Successive discounts of 20% and 10%: (-20) + (-10) + (200/100) = -30 + 2 = -28%
Price increased by 25% then decreased by 20%: (+25) + (-20) - (500/100) = 5 - 5 = 0% (No change)
🧠 English Alphabet Forward & Reverse Positions (EJOTY + 27 Rule)

Forward anchors: E(5), J(10), O(15), T(20), Y(25).
Reverse position rule: Reverse Rank = 27 − Forward Rank.

Opposite Pairs: A-Z, B-Y, C-X, D-W, E-V, F-U, G-T, H-S, I-R, J-Q, K-P, L-O, M-N (Sum is always 27!)
📊 Syllogisms Definitive Conclusion Rules
• All A are B + All B are C ⇒ All A are C, Some A are C.
• Some A are B + All B are C ⇒ Some A are C.
• No A is B + All B are C ⇒ Some C are not A.
• Only a few A are B ⇒ Both "Some A are B" AND "Some A are NOT B" are 100% TRUE!
🏦 Banking Statutory Ratios & RBI Prudential Framework
• CRR (Cash Reserve Ratio): Percentage of NDTL banks must hold with RBI in cash. No interest earned.
• SLR (Statutory Liquidity Ratio): Percentage of NDTL held in approved gold, cash, or G-Secs.
• Repo Rate: Rate at which RBI lends short-term funds to banks against collateral.
• SDF (Standing Deposit Facility): RBI absorbs liquidity without giving government collateral (placed 25 bps below Repo).
• MSF (Marginal Standing Facility): Overnight lending for penal emergency liquidity (25 bps above Repo).
