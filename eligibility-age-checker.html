❓ Frequently Asked Questions: Age Limits & Eligibility

What is the age cutoff reference date for UPSC CSE? UPSC Civil Services Examination calculates candidate age as of 1st August of the examination year. Candidates must be between 21 and 32 years of age for the General/EWS category.
What is the age limit for Banking Exams (SBI PO vs Clerk)? For SBI PO and IBPS PO, candidates must be between 21 and 30 years. For SBI Clerk and IBPS Clerk, the age window is 20 to 28 years as of the notification cutoff date.
