Exam Architecture • CBT Strategy

How This CBT Mock Test Simulator Works

Modern Indian banking and aptitude exams (SBI PO, IBPS Clerk, RRB, RBI Assistant) use Computer-Based Tests (CBT) powered by the TCS-iON delivery platform. This simulator reproduces the exact operational mechanics:

⏱️ Sectional 20-Min Lock Forces you to make rapid attempt decisions. You cannot switch sections until the countdown runs out, mimicking official exam pressure.
🎯 5-Option Elimination Banking questions contain 5 choices rather than 4, introducing subtle distractor traps and "Cannot Be Determined" options.
🧮 Negative Marking Penalty Calculates live gross vs. net marks, enforcing a -0.25 mark deduction per wrong answer to prevent wild guesswork.
